# Publication rules

- Publish machine-specific writeups only after the Hack The Box machine has officially retired.
- Do not include anything about active machines or anything that spoils a box that has not retired.
- Verify retirement before adding technical details; if status is uncertain, leave a clearly marked non-spoiler TODO stub.
- Sanitise every writeup: no flags, credentials, hashes, personal identifiers in screenshots, or other sensitive artifacts.
- Do not guess. Mark unverified specifics TODO or omit them.
- Keep the scope to HTB writeups and learning notes; do not include school, subject, exam, or grade content.
